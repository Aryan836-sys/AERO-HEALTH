The six-category thresholds are based on AirNow's US AQI categories. The app's illustrative risk scoring is not an AirNow or medical model. All measurements, source assignments, forecasts and community incidents in the fixtures are fictional. Place names/approximate coordinates serve a frontend demonstration, not a certified monitoring-station list.

The code, original Aero Health SVG mark, CSS artwork, and demo dataset were authored for this delivery. Third-party dependencies remain under their own licenses and are obtained by npm; no vendor binaries, font files, reference-image assets, or test-only substitute libraries are bundled.
